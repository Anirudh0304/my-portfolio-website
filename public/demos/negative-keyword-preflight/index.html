What this check can tell you

It applies a bounded text simulation to the terms you supply, up to the 16-word boundary. It does not normalize misspellings or model every symbol, campaign/list attachment, or eligibility setting. A “no direct match” result is not proof that Google will treat the terms as distinct or serve an ad.
